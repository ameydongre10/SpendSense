Analytical Methodology

We don't optimize for accuracy.
We optimize for precision under risk.

In credit card payments, reporting 99.8% raw accuracy is trivial and dangerous: a model predicting zero fraud achieves 99.83% accuracy while losing 100% of compromised card volume.

SpendSense utilizes a two-tier classification pipeline. We establish a linear baseline via balanced Logistic Regression, followed by an optimized XGBoost model parameterized with scale_pos_weight = 577.3 to penalize false negatives proportionally. The result preserves 98.98% recall while slashing false-positive customer friction by 64%.
